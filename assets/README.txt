How to add your own files (keep the same file names):
  logo.png   - your logo (square, transparent background works best)
  photo.jpg  - your picture (portrait, about 800 x 960)

In index.html, also replace:
  you@example.com      (appears 3 times)
  8800000000000 and +880 0000-000000   (WhatsApp)
  the # links for Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube

The contact form opens the visitor's email app. It does not send by itself.
